## Themes

- **Scale-across is the new frontier**: multiple sessions (Shainer's roadmap, the multi-gigawatt panel, NVL72 deep dives, Spectrum-X plugins in the NCCL talk) treat multi-site, latency-predictable, lossless interconnect of federated AI factories as the defining network problem after scale-up/scale-out.
- **Optics are moving into the package**: co-packaged optics on both Quantum-X InfiniBand and Spectrum-X Ethernet signals the shift away from pluggable-dominated DC optics at AI scale — a structural change for anyone buying DC optics and cabling.
- **Security is moving into the DPU and the fabric**: DOCA security microservices, BlueField line-rate policy, and DPU-based tenant isolation recast network security as offload rather than host agents — with an explicit pitch that security must not tax AI "tokenomics."
- **The agentic trust gap is now named openly**: the OpenClaw panel, DOCA's agentic-workflow security, and the verifiable-AI runtime all concede that governance and identity infrastructure for autonomous agents doesn't exist at scale — a clear engagement window for identity-focused work (credential scoping, attestation, agent-to-agent trust).
- **Failure engineering is a first-class infrastructure discipline**: Lablup's NCCL/storage/precision failures, ByteDance's 97% effective-training-time run, PyTorch elasticity, and shift-left digital twins collectively show the field treating fault tolerance, fault localization, and pre-hardware validation as core design inputs, not afterthoughts.
- **Disaggregated inference is driving a second communication-stack evolution**: NIXL's async transfers across HBM/DRAM/storage tiers, KV-cache movement, Alibaba's PD-disagg Kubernetes orchestration, and Torchcomms' vendor-neutral layer all point to inference datapaths — not training collectives — as where comm-stack innovation is concentrating.
