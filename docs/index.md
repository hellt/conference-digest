# Conference Digest

<div class="cd-hero" markdown>

**The talks and papers worth your time, picked from full conference programs.**

The pipeline tracks networking, security and identity conferences, from SIGCOMM and NSDI to IETF, KubeCon and Netdev. After an event ends and its program is published, ToFaRev reads all of it: hundreds of talks, or more than a thousand drafts. Then it writes a short digest with **Core** picks, **Adjacent** context, a few **Wildcards**, and the **Themes** of the cycle.
